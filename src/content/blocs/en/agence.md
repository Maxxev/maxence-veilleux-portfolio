professionals, community groups. Mostly rebuilds — a site that has aged and
needs redoing without losing its content or its search ranking.

and content in typed collections, or **WordPress** when the client needs to
manage everything themselves. I've also worked on several AI-augmented internal
tools, described in my projects as far as I'm able to discuss them.

Several of those sites aren't live yet. They'll appear here when they are.
