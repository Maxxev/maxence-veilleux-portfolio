I'm a **Computer Science Technology** student at Cégep de Sherbrooke, in the
Design and Programming stream, and alongside my studies I build websites and
internal tools for real clients.

What interests me in this work is the moment a constraint forces a better
decision. Having to serve two clients from one API forces you to settle the
data contracts before designing any screens. Wanting a client to review what an
AI proposes forces you to produce readable diffs. Good structures almost always
come from that rather than from an intention to be elegant.

This site is a modest example: it's built on the same SCSS foundation as the
sites I deliver at the agency, and its dark mode required no theme-specific
rule in any component.
